Welcome

A smart city uses technology, data and community participation to make urban life safer, cleaner and more efficient. This website provides information about smart city initiatives, public services, transportation and sustainable urban development.

Smart City Initiatives

  • Integrated Command Centre: One control room for traffic, emergency, utility and surveillance data.
  • Smart Street Lighting: Adaptive LED lights that save energy.
  • Public Wi-Fi Zones: Free internet in parks, bus stations and markets.
  • Smart Infrastructure: GIS mapping and digital planning for roads, drainage and housing.

Smart Transportation

Integrated Public Transit

  • Real-time bus and metro tracking
  • One smart card for all public transport
  • Dedicated bus lanes

Intelligent Traffic Management

  • Adaptive traffic signals
  • Automated traffic violation detection
  • Smart parking with live slot availability

Electric Mobility

  • Public EV charging stations
  • Electric buses replacing diesel fleets
  • Battery-swapping hubs for e-rickshaws

Sustainable Urban Development

  • Solar Rooftops: Clean power from municipal buildings and housing societies.
  • Waste Management: Segregation, recycling and waste-to-energy plants.
  • Water Recycling: Treated wastewater reused for gardens and industry.
  • Green Spaces: More parks, tree cover and urban forests.

Development Roadmap

  1. Phase 1 - Foundation: fibre network, command centre, sensors, digital portal.
  2. Phase 2 - Mobility: smart signals, e-buses, charging network, transit card.
  3. Phase 3 - Sustainability: solar expansion, waste-to-energy, water recycling.
  4. Phase 4 - Citizen Experience: AI helpdesk, personalised services, open data.
